## English

The **Now lens follows the top band selector** (#1300): its internal title and band toggle are gone - flip 2.4/5 GHz on the toolbar and the survey follows. The client table sorts band and SSID with a signal-descending tiebreak (strongest neighbors grouped on top), adds a minimum-signal filter (≥ -70/-80/-90 dBm), and linked MACs (#1151) are searchable and visible in the expanded detail. Also restores the sticky band across APs that a working-session merge had partially dropped, and the CI snapshot now builds on every main push (#1271).

## Español

**La lente "Canales actuales" sigue al selector superior** (#1300): su título y toggle de banda internos desaparecen - cambias 2.4/5 GHz en la barra y el survey la sigue.
