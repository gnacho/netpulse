## Control over the SSID impostor rule

A rule that cries wolf is worse than no rule. From the alert row, the shield button now approves a flagged BSSID as your own: the alert resolves on the spot, the approval persists, and that MAC never trips the rule again (you can revoke it from the approved list). And if you would rather not hear about impostors at all, the alerts settings has a global switch for the whole rule - turning it off also resolves the active impostor alerts. (#1400, #1401)

## The rack view graduates out of Labs

The rack gets its own always-visible toggle in Settings > Services (still off by default), the nav item drops the LABS badge and no longer requires Labs, and the caption no longer says "do not use in production". (#1407, #1408)

## Also in this release

- #1408: a handful of strings that rendered as raw keys now show real text in both languages: the rack uplink unit selector and its confirmation toast, the close button in Roaming, and the vendor-repository warning in the owut upgrade confirmation.
- #1403, #1405: the public landing folds its feature cards into compact rows with a one-line hook (the details unfold on click) and the install section goes full width.
- #1404, #1406: the English landing screenshots now show the app UI in English, and the demo dataset is advertised as its real 65 devices.
