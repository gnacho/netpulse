# v2.28.2

Server + frontend. The agent is unchanged (AGENT_VERSION stays 2.26.11).

**EN**:
- Channel analysis: the X axis now shows every channel of the active band (2.4: 1-13; 5: UNII-1..4), including empty ones, like LuCI; your own managed network is drawn as a violet mountain on its in-use channel at -25 dBm (it is not present in external scans); and one band is shown at a time (2.4 GHz / 5 GHz toggle, no "All") (#542, #554).
- Device actions: the DHCP reservation (GET/PUT/DELETE /api/devices/{mac}/reservation) now targets the router that serves the device's lease, not always the global gateway. On a separate LAN whose DHCP is granted by another router (an AP/lab with its own dnsmasq, e.g. rt-lab on 192.168.2.x), the static host section was being written to the wrong router. When no lease is known it falls back to the gateway, as before (#537).

**ES**:
- Análisis de canales: el eje X muestra ahora todos los canales de la banda activa (2.4: 1-13; 5: UNII-1..4), incluidos los vacíos, como en LuCI; la red gestionada se dibuja como una montaña violeta en su canal en uso a -25 dBm (no aparece en los escaneos externos); y se muestra una sola banda a la vez (selector 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz, sin "Todos") (#542, #554).
- Acciones de dispositivo: la reserva DHCP (GET/PUT/DELETE /api/devices/{mac}/reservation) ahora apunta al router que sirve el lease del dispositivo, no siempre al gateway global. En una LAN separada cuyo DHCP lo concede otro router (un AP/lab con su propio dnsmasq, p. ej. rt-lab en 192.168.2.x), la sección host estática se escribía en el router equivocado. Cuando no hay lease conocido cae al gateway, como antes (#537).
